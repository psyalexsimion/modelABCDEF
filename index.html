A. Activating Event

Question 1 of 3

What happened?
💡 Describe the activating event objectively. Focus on the facts of what happened.
Who was involved?
💡 List the people who were part of this situation.
Where were you?
💡 Describe the location or setting.
What was your automatic thought or interpretation of the situation?
💡 Identify the thoughts that immediately came to mind during the situation.
What assumptions were you making?
💡 What beliefs or assumptions underlie your automatic thoughts?
What does this mean about you, others, or the world?
💡 What deeper meanings or beliefs does this situation reveal?
How did you feel emotionally (e.g., sad, angry, anxious)?
💡 Name the emotions you experienced. It's okay to have multiple feelings.
What did you do or want to do (e.g., withdraw, confront them)?
💡 Describe your behavioral response or urges. What actions did you take or feel compelled to take?
Challenge the negative belief.
💡 What evidence contradicts it? Is there another way to look at the situation? Are your assumptions realistic?
Develop a more balanced and realistic thought.
💡 How can you think about the situation differently that is more helpful?
Which cognitive distortions apply?
💡 Select all patterns of thinking that match your experience. It's common to have multiple.
